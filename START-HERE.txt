HOW TO VIEW YOUR WEBSITE

1. Extract the entire ZIP first (right-click > Extract All).
2. Open the extracted mahadev-properties folder.
3. Double-click OPEN-WEBSITE.cmd to open the homepage in your default browser.
   Alternatively, right-click index.html > Open with > Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
4. Keep assets, style.css, script.js and all pages together. Do not open only index.html from inside the ZIP.

A Git comparison window displays source code, not the rendered website. An LF/CRLF line-ending warning does not prevent a browser from opening HTML.

For hosting, upload the website files to your domain web root. OPEN-WEBSITE.cmd is for Windows local preview only; do not upload it. Google Sheet lead capture still requires activation as explained in README.txt.
