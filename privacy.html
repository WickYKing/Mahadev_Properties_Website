Privacy notice

The enquiry form asks for your name, mobile number, property requirement, location, optional budget and message, and your permission to contact you. When you save an enquiry online, we also record a reference number, the time, the page you sent it from, the page that referred you and any campaign parameters in the web address.

Why we collect these details

Mahadev Properties uses these details to understand and respond to your property enquiry by phone or WhatsApp. Saved enquiries are stored in the business’s private Google Sheet for follow-up, and access is limited to the people handling enquiries.

WhatsApp and Google

If you choose WhatsApp, your message is sent only when you tap Send in WhatsApp. Google and WhatsApp process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The map on the contact page loads content from Google.
