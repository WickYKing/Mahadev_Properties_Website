YOUR PROPERTY ENQUIRY GUIDE

Know what to ask before your next property visit.

A few clear details can help you avoid unsuitable options and make your discussion with a property dealer more useful.

Discuss your requirement

BEFORE YOU TAKE THE NEXT STEP

A better enquiry starts with better details.

01

Define your requirement

Choose sale, purchase, rent or collaboration. Note the property type, location, approximate size and your timing.

02

Discuss budget & availability

Share a realistic budget or expected value. Ask about current options, visit arrangements and any applicable brokerage.

03

Review before committing

Check the property, relevant documents and agreed terms with qualified professionals before making a payment or signing.

Questions to ask during the conversation

01

Does it fit my intended use?

Discuss the location, size, access and whether the property is suitable for your intended residential or commercial use. Confirm the details for the specific property.

02

What costs and terms should I confirm?

Ask for the asking price or rent, applicable brokerage and the proposed payment or tenancy terms. Get agreed terms in writing.

03

What remains to be checked?

Arrange a visit and ask what information is available. Relevant property documents and commitments should be reviewed by qualified professionals.
