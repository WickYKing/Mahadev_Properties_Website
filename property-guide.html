Before you make an enquiry

  1. Define your requirement

    Decide on sale, purchase, rent or collaboration. Note the property type, location, approximate size and timing.

  2. Discuss budget and availability

    Share a realistic budget or expected price. Ask about current options, visits and any brokerage.

  3. Review before committing

    Check the property, documents and agreed terms with qualified professionals before paying or signing.

Questions to ask during the conversation

Does it fit my intended use?

Discuss location, size, access and whether the property suits your residential or commercial use. Confirm details for the specific property.

Which costs and terms should I confirm?

Ask for the asking price or rent, brokerage, and the proposed payment or tenancy terms. Get agreed terms in writing.

What still needs to be checked?

Arrange a visit and ask what information is available. Property documents and commitments should be reviewed by qualified professionals.
